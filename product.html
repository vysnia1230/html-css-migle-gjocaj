Product information

Built for the long way round

The Nordland is a waterproof outdoor jacket designed for hiking and changing weather. Its layered construction helps keep out rain and wind while remaining comfortable during active use.

The adjustable hood, practical pockets and relaxed fit make it easy to wear with additional layers on colder days.

  • Helmet-compatible storm hood with three-point adjustment
  • Two chest pockets and two hand pockets, all pack-strap compatible
  • Pit zips with two-way sliders for fast venting on the climb
  • Removable waist belt and adjustable hem drawcord
  • PFAS-free durable water repellent finish
  • Recycled 70D face fabric
